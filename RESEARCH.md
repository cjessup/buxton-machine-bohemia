# Buxton Machine redesign research

Live site fetched 2026-11-23 from the apex host `https://buxtonmachine.com/` (home and `default.htm` are the same FrontPage page) and `https://buxtonmachine.com/machined%20parts.htm`.

Line of business on those pages: a machine shop and welding shop in Bohemia, Long Island, NY. CNC and manual milling, drilling, cutting, tapping, and turning. Welding is in house: TIG, MIG, stick, and plasma cutting. Metals and most plastics. Prototype through production quantities, fixtures, and assemblies, from prints or with help when no drawing exists.

## Palette, type, and imagery taken from the live site

- Page maroon `#800000`, sidebar green `#339966`, content cream `#FFFF99`, body text `#333333`. Parts page body is gray `#808080` with maroon `#800000` type.
- Faces named in the markup: Andy, Bookman Old Style, Baskerville Old Face, Copperplate Gothic Bold / Light, Century Schoolbook.
- Mark: `wpe1.gif` (BUXTON / MACHINE / TON wordmark in maroon, white, and black) and `test_star_3.jpg` (maroon and black star on yellow).
- Work photos already on the home page: `redweb.jpg`, `springerweb.jpg`, `footweb.jpg`, `weldweb.jpg`, `customweb.jpg`, `greenweb.jpg`. Saved under `assets/`. They are small, so the demo does not blow them up into a full-bleed hero. The logo sits uncovered with the headline under it.

The home “Machined Parts” link points at a Desktop path (`../../Desktop/NEW WEB2/machined parts.htm`), not at the live parts page. This demo links to `machined-parts.html`.

Visible email on both pages is `nyle@buxtonmachine.com`. The home `mailto` matches that address. The parts-page `mailto` is `Nybuxt@aol.com`. Those two targets disagree, so the demo uses the visible site-domain address and does not treat the AOL address as a second official email.

Phone digits are the same on both pages. Home writes `631 - 218 - 2791` and `631 - 218 - 2792`. The parts page writes `631-218-2791` and `631-218-2792`. Each page keeps the spacing from that page. Address is shown once, as 289 Knickerbocker Ave, Bohemia, NY 11716.

No hours and no copyright year appear on the live pages, so none are added. Directory listings that mention a founding year, equipment brands, headcount, or hours were not used.

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photos, or branding copied.

### 1. Rand Machine Products

https://www.randmachine.com/

CNC machining and welding/fabrication shop in Falconer, NY.

Borrowed:

- The first screen separates machining from welding and fabrication, instead of one undifferentiated block.
- Industries are a compact row of names under the work, not a long essay.
- A quote / contact action sits next to the capability description.

### 2. Williamson Machine

https://www.williamson-machine.com/

CNC and manual machine shop with in-house MIG, TIG, and stick welding in Jacksonville, FL.

Borrowed:

- The phone number stays in the top bar as a tap target and is repeated with the street address.
- Capabilities are short labeled groups (CNC and manual, then welding) with tight lists.
- Call and a contact action sit side by side.

### 3. Piedmont Precision Machine

https://www.ppmmach.com/

Machine shop in Danville, VA, with manual machining plus a fabrication and welding department, and a shop-photo gallery.

Borrowed:

- Shop photos are a real gallery that opens larger, not a strip of tiny images with no way to look at them.
- Industry names are a plain list.
- Phone and fax are published together where someone would actually call.
